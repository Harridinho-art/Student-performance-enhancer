# Aree Student Performance Enhancer 📊

## Overview
First and foremost, a very special thank you to my former employer, Sizwe Msimango (Founder of Aree), for allowing me to showcase the work that I did for him during my time at Aree to every recruiter who will be viewing my CV/Resume. With respect to sensitive information, I can only showcase so much.

This showcase is a project that I was tasked with, to track student performance and correlate it to not only attendance in class, but also how to keep learners engaged in consuming knowledge. It explores what's the best way to get them more involved in learning and understanding whichever particular subject they're struggling with.

## Main Objectives
* **Track and Assess:** The main objectives were to track student attendance and their performance for a particular period of time. 
* **Analyze Data:** With the help of the teachers from the local school, we were able to accurately assess the data and find the correlation between attendance and performance. 
* **Improve Engagement:** As per the teachers' request, the aim was not to improve attendance for their classes, but to instead find a way to keep learners engaged and to make learning more interactive, especially for subjects that the majority of the learners struggled with.

## My Role and Contributions
This was one of the biggest projects I was tasked with during my time at Aree. My core responsibilities included:
* **Data Management & Reporting:** I was tasked with cleaning and organising the data, as well as building a report for the teachers so they could analyse where to improve and what to do moving forward. 
* **Course Design:** After compiling all the data and turning it into useful information, I was then tasked with creating content/courses, specifically for grades 8 & 9, and making them very interactive. 
* **Creative Autonomy:** I was given the freedom to design the courses in the best way possible. This was also one of the first times I was given the freedom to design an entire course by myself. 
* **Project Output:** I designed 8 courses, 5 being Economics and Accounting (EMS) courses, and the other 3 being Maths courses.
